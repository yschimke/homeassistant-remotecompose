Terrazzo is a dashboard client for Home Assistant, on your phone, tablet
and Wear OS watch. You configure your own Home Assistant URL and
authenticate via IndieAuth / OAuth2; the app renders your existing
dashboards using Jetpack RemoteCompose.

On your phone and tablet:
- Your existing Home Assistant dashboards, with live entity values
- Light and dark themes
- Choose which cards appear on your watch

On your Wear OS watch:
- Browse your dashboards and pinned cards with live values
- Five configurable watch widgets and tiles, each in a small or large size
- Keeps showing the last synced values while your watch is offline

The watch app syncs from the paired phone app, so set up Home Assistant
on your phone first.

This is a single-developer hobby project; you can read all the source
code at https://github.com/yschimke/homeassistant-remotecompose.

Notes:
- The developer collects nothing. The app talks only to the Home
  Assistant server you configure.
- No analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDKs.
- Authentication tokens stay on your device.
