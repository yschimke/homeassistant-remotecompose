Terrazzo

Home Assistant dashboards, drawn with Terrazzo's cards.

Is this your Wasmo?

This app only opens for its owner. Sign in to your Wasmo, then open Home Assistant from your launcher.

Once it's open you can install it to your phone or desktop: use your browser's Install app or Add to Home Screen.

Install Terrazzo on your own Wasmo

  1. In your Wasmo, open the computer menu and choose Install App.
  2. Paste this address: https://github.com/yschimke/homeassistant-remotecompose/releases/latest/download/terrazzo.wasmo
  3. Name it (for example terrazzo), open it, and sign in with Home Assistant.

Home Assistant must be reachable over HTTPS from your browser (Nabu Casa or your own TLS).

Other ways to use Terrazzo

  1. In your browser, with nothing to install.
  2. Inside Home Assistant, as a wall panel and Lovelace cards: integration guide.
  3. On the desktop: Linux, Windows and macOS installers.
