Every preview across every player: its baked PNG (the offline Robolectric/Skiko render) next to the same ir/*.rc document as each player renders it. The JS player is the vendored TypeScript RC.RcdPlayer on a <canvas>. Nothing is diffed until you ask for it: pick a column in Diff against and every other column grows a pixel diff plus a mismatch chip. Choosing the baked PNG replays the build-time pixelmatch diffs; choosing a player diffs in the browser, which is how you compare two players directly. Rows sort worst-match-first using the build-time scores in the header. A preview whose baked PNG is fully transparent is shown but not scored against it: with nothing in the reference, a player that draws nothing would score a perfect 0% — so those rows read no reference and stay out of the means.
